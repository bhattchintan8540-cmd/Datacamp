Recommendations
P1: Fix Free-plan onboarding and the path to paid
  Issue: Free churn is 41.0% (43 of 105 customers), above the Basic rate of 23.7%.
  - Review the first-week Free experience for drop-off before the first activity.
  - Add a time-boxed prompt that converts active Free users to Basic.
  - Track Free churn weekly against the Basic churn rate.
  Owner: Growth | Timeline: 30 days
  Impact: Closing the gap with Basic on the 62 active Free customers is about 10.7 additional retained accounts. Revenue follows only if those accounts convert to a paid plan.

P2: Re-engage active low and no-engagement customers
  Issue: Low engagement churns at 35.7% and No Engagement at 37.5%. 41 active customers are still in those two levels, including 26 on a paid plan.
  - Open a save playbook for the 41 active Low and No Engagement customers.
  - Prioritize the paid subset, where a retained account still has monthly revenue.
  - Pair outreach with a single activation task rather than a discount by default.
  Owner: Customer Success | Timeline: 14 days
  Impact: Moving the paid low/no-engagement churn rate from 25.7% toward 20.0% is about 1.5 saved customers, $81 MRR, and $1563 gross-margin LTV (24 months, 80% margin).

P3: Nurture the Medium engagement cohort
  Issue: Medium engagement holds 74 of 114 churned customers (64.9%) at a 32.0% rate. 157 Medium customers are still active.
  - Build a lifecycle track for Medium customers who have not added a second activity.
  - Flag Medium accounts whose support tickets are rising.
  - Set a target to move Medium churn toward the High-engagement rate.
  Owner: Lifecycle Marketing | Timeline: 45 days
  Impact: A nurture program on the active Medium group addresses the largest slice of historical churn volume, even though its rate is below Low and No Engagement.

P4: Increase product activity before accounts go quiet
  Issue: Churned customers average 0.50 activities versus 1.35 for active customers (gap -0.85). Support tickets average 2.80 among churned customers and 2.10 among active customers.
  - Instrument an activation checklist and alert when a paid account stays at zero activities.
  - Route high-ticket, low-activity accounts to support before renewal.
  - Do not use average resolution time alone to score risk; the separation is modest.
  Owner: Product | Timeline: 60 days
  Impact: Raising product activity among quiet accounts attacks the strongest simple association in this workbook. Resolution time is only a mild companion signal and should not be treated as a churn label.
